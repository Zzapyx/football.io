Football

What is Football?

Football, is the world's most popular sport, played by two teams of 11 players on a rectangular grass or turf field. The main objective is to score points by maneuvering a round ball into the opposing team's goal using any part of the body except the hands and arms, with the goalkeeper being the only player permitted to use their hands within a designated box. A standard professional match lasts 90 minutes, divided into two 45-minute halves, and is controlled by a central referee. Globally, the sport is governed by FIFA, and its ultimate tournament is the FIFA World Cup, which takes place every four years and stands as the most-watched sporting event on Earth.

Rules of Football

  1. Each team has 11 players, including a goalkeeper.
  2. The main objective is to score more goals than the opposing team.
  3. A standard match lasts 90 minutes, divided into two 45-minute halves.
  4. Players cannot deliberately touch the ball with their hands or arms, except the goalkeeper in their own penalty area.
  5. The ball is out of play when it completely crosses the touchline or goal line, or when the referee stops play.
  6. A goal is scored when the whole ball crosses the goal line between the goalposts and under the crossbar.
  7. Fouls, such as kicking, pushing, or tripping an opponent unfairly, can result in a free kick or penalty kick.
  8. A penalty kick is awarded for certain direct-free-kick offences committed by a player inside their own penalty area while the ball is in play.
  9. A yellow card is a warning, while a red card means the player is sent off.
  10. A player can be penalized for offside if they become involved in active play while in an offside position when a teammate plays the ball.
  11. A throw-in is awarded when the ball completely crosses a touchline.
  12. A goal kick is awarded when the attacking team last touches the ball before it crosses the defending team's goal line without a goal being scored.
  13. A corner kick is awarded when the defending team last touches the ball before it crosses their own goal line without a goal being scored.
  14. The referee is responsible for enforcing the rules and controlling the match.
